# Refined 2 — Layered panes

**Idea.** The single window splits into two tall panes sliding past each other: green (off-peak) behind at upper left, red (peak) in front at lower right. A constant 32 px navy gap is cut out of the green by the red's outline, so there is no stroke, shadow or fourth shape. The offset makes the overlap read like a sliding door or a pane passing through a frame, with the crisp top-right corner of the green giving it a stepped, doorway-like edge.

**Changed from concept 1.** The diagonal slit is gone; the split is now an overlap with a concave, rounded cut. Fields are separate panes instead of one window. Still tile + 2 shapes, no gradient.

**Glyph.** Back pane cut into an L by the front pane's gap, plus the solid front pane (18 pt grid, gap 1.2 pt, front pane 8–15 × 5–16).

**16 px check.** Icon: the two colour masses and the offset are clear at 16 px and the navy gap survives as a dark seam; it is the least fine-detailed of the three. Glyph: legible at 16 px, but its silhouette resembles a generic "copy / duplicate" symbol, which is the main weakness of this direction.

**Light/dark.** Navy tile holds on both. Glyph is solid and template-safe.
