# Refined 1 — Authored slit

**Idea.** The generic diagonal cut from concept 1 becomes a stepped notch: the slit runs flat, climbs one 45° step and runs flat again, like a price line ticking up between off-peak (green, above) and peak (red, below). The gap has a constant 52 px width (mitred offsets, not a thick stroke), and the two fields are symmetric about the window centre, so the step reads as drawn rather than defaulted. The negative space is a price-step/tick shape; it does not form a "C".

**Changed from concept 1.** Straight diagonal replaced by flat–45°–flat step; slit is the same width everywhere; same navy tile, window (460×560, r105) and green/red fields. Still tile + 2 shapes, no gradient.

**Glyph.** The same window as two solid pieces split by the same step, on an 18 pt grid (window 3–15 × 2–16, r3, gap 1.5 pt).

**16 px check.** Icon: the green/red masses and the step stay distinct; the slit renders at roughly 0.8 px, so it is a dark seam rather than a crisp line, and the tile is about 13 px wide. Glyph: at 16 px the 1.5 pt gap is about 1.3 px and the step is still visible, but the whole mark can read as an "S" or "Z" at a glance.

**Light/dark.** Navy tile holds on light and dark backgrounds. The template glyph is solid black/white, so it works on both menu-bar appearances.
