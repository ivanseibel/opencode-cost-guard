# Concept 3 — Gate

A solid navy monolith on an off-white tile with a tall arched slot cut through to the base and lit green: a threshold you step through when the price window opens. The single-mass architecture and the one green opening are the whole idea; the glyph is the same monolith with a transparent doorway, which makes it the simplest menu-bar silhouette of the three. It is distinctive because it carries no literal chart, coin or shield, though it has less price-window meaning and can be read as a generic door or power slot. 16 px check: the door stays visible and the glyph is very strong, but the app icon has a weakness — the off-white tile nearly disappears on light backgrounds at 16 px, leaving only the navy block and green slit floating, so it needs a tile edge or a navy tile if chosen.
