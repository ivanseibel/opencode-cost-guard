# Concept 1 — Price window

A rounded window split by a single diagonal slit into a green field (off-peak) and a red field (peak), on a deep-navy tile. The slit reads like a price line crossing the window, so the icon says "timing and price windows" instead of "security"; the monochrome glyph keeps the same rounded window and slit as two solid pieces. It is distinctive because the diagonal cut keeps it from looking like a generic shield, a toggle, or a flat two-column split. 16 px check: honest result is good — the green and red masses stay clearly separate and the diagonal survives as a visible colour change, though the 40 px slit itself softens to a blurry boundary (about 0.6 px) and the tile corners are soft; at 16 px the glyph's 1.4 pt slit is the only fine detail and is borderline but still visible.
